### Rules — read these first

These matter because a real business will publish this and be judged on it.

| Never | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Invent a testimonial, review, rating or customer name | Fabricated reviews are illegal in most jurisdictions (FTC in the US, CMA in the UK). Only use quotes that appear in the harvested data. |
| Invent licence numbers, certifications, insurance or accreditations | Claiming credentials a business does not hold exposes them to real liability. |
| Invent prices, guarantees, warranties or response times | These are contractual promises. If it is not in the source, leave it out. |
| Invent years in business, staff counts or job numbers | "Serving since 1987" must come from the source, not from a nice round number. |
| Invent an address, service area or phone number | Wrong contact details lose leads and break local SEO. |

Anything you would like to claim but cannot verify goes in `_meta.todo` as a question for the business owner. That list becomes your client kick-off call.

**Do** rewrite freely for tone, clarity, structure and length. Turning "WELCOME TO OUR WEBSITE" into a benefit-led headline is the entire point of this step.

## What to write

### 1. Hero

Current headline: **Welcome to Brannigan Heating & Air**

Write `content.hero.headline` as a benefit the customer cares about, not a greeting. Ten words maximum. Then `content.hero.subhead`: one or two sentences naming what they do, who for, and where.

Also set `content.hero.primaryCta` (`{ "label": "...", "href": "contact/" }`) — say what happens, e.g. "Get a free estimate", not "Submit".
