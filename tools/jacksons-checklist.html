The original logo file

The logo we’re using was lifted off a photo of a printed advert card. It’s only 256 pixels square — about the size of a postage stamp on screen — so it goes soft and fuzzy if we make it any bigger.

How to track it down

  1. Think back to whoever made your van signwriting, your business cards or that advert card. Whoever designed it will still have the original.
  2. Message them and ask for “the original logo artwork, ideally an SVG, or a large PNG with a transparent background”. Those words will mean something to them even if they don’t to you.
  3. Send us whatever they send you, even if you’re not sure it’s right.

If nobody has it, say so — we can have it redrawn properly instead.

All on your phone

4
Biggest win on this list

Get your Google review link

You have seven reviews and every one is five stars. Reviews are the single biggest thing that decides whether Google shows you above the next stonemason. There’s a link that opens the review box for a customer in one tap.

On your phone

  1. Open the Google Maps app, signed in as the account that owns the business listing.
  2. Tap your profile picture, top right.
  3. Tap Your Business Profile.
  4. Look for Ask for reviews (sometimes “Get more reviews”).
  5. Tap Copy link and send it to us.

Then text that link to every customer when you finish a job. It costs nothing and it’s the best marketing a trade business has.

On your phone

Two minutes

Your opening hours don’t match

Google shows your business as opening at 6am. The website says 8am to 5pm, Monday to Friday. One of them is wrong and we don’t know which — so tell us, and we’ll make the site agree with you rather than guess.

It matters more than it sounds. Google checks whether a business says the same thing about itself everywhere, and treats a business that contradicts itself as less trustworthy — which feeds into whether you show up in the map results at all. And a customer who reads 6am somewhere might ring you at half six.

If 8am is right, fix it on Google — on your phone

  1. Open the Google Maps app, signed in as the account that owns the business listing.
  2. Tap your profile picture, top right.
  3. Tap Your Business Profile.
  4. Tap Edit profile, then Hours.
  5. Set the days and times, and save.

If 6am is right — and for a stonemason starting early that would not surprise us — do nothing at all. Just tell us, and we’ll change the website to say 6am instead.
