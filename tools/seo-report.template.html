What Google has indexed

A page that is not in the index cannot rank for anything, however well it is written. This is the ceiling every other effort sits under.

Where the sites rank today

Read by hand off google.co.uk, logged out, one reading per query. Organic is the position among the ordinary blue links; map is the position in the local pack, which for a local trade is the part people actually tap.

How much to trust these. Google personalises and localises results, so a search from this machine is not what a customer standing in Basingstoke or Cheltenham sees. Treat them as one honest reading rather than a rank-tracking service. Google Search Console reports the real positions people saw, averaged over everyone who searched — which is the other reason to get the sites registered on it.
